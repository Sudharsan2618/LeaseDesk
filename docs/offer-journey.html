Deterministic pipeline

engine · rules decide

The confirmed requirement is frozen and priced by policy DE_PKW_V1 — no AI in this path. Ordered stages:

completeness→ customer context→ residual→ eligibility→ asset assessment→ scoring G/Y/R→ funding→ margin→ calculate
hard blockSanctions match · KYC failed · insolvency · asset assessment unavailable · special payment >30% · price >150k → RED, offer BLOCKED (terminal).
reviewSoft flags (YELLOW risk, high-value >100k, low residual confidence, mileage/special-payment review, manual scoring) → priced, but need human review.

Scenarios & explanation

engine + agent

Engine builds variants around the requested term (nearest valid terms) honoring the customer's choices, adds an all-inclusive bundle when services are open, tags budget-fit. The agent then writes a grounded, numbers-based explanation.

Mandatory human review

Required before any offer is generated (BR-15). The human sees the complete offer + all exceptions and decides. Generation is blocked here if the outcome is BLOCKED.

Final outcome

engine

Recomputes the outcome: READY · REQUIRES_ACTION · BLOCKED. Only a non-blocked, human-confirmed offer proceeds to generate.

The math — engine formulas (DE_PKW_V1)

Residual value (DE_PKW_RV_V1)

RV%base_term_RV + mileage_adj + age_adj, clamp[20,80]
Base RV by term12→80 · 24→68 · 36→58 · 48→49 · 60→41 %
Mileage adj (base 15,000)−2.5pp / +5000km (cap −15) · +1.5pp / −5000km (cap +5)
RV amountlist_price_net × RV%
ConfidenceHIGH / MEDIUM / LOW → funding spread + term cap

Rate build-up

Funding ratereference_rate + term_spread + asset_uncertainty_spread
Term spread0.35 / 0.50 / 0.70 / 0.90 pp by term band
Asset spreadHIGH 0 · MEDIUM 0.30 · LOW 0.60 pp
Commercial margin1.50 + risk_adj + deal_size_adj, clamp[1.00,3.50]
Customer finance ratefunding_rate + commercial_margin

Lease calculation (MVP_LEASE_CALC_V1)

Monthly rate rfinance_rate / 100 / 12
NetCapacq + financed_fees − discount − special_payment
PV(residual)RV_amount / (1+r)^n
Base lease(NetCap − PV_RV) × r / (1 − (1+r)^−n)
+ services / VATmaint €39 · tyres €19 · ins 3.5%/yr of list ÷12 · ×(1+VAT)
Guard: if NetCap ≤ PV(residual) → special payment too high → blocked.

Risk scoring (DE_PKW_RISK_V1)

Hard blocks → REDsanctions match · KYC failed · insolvency
Weighted 0–100credit .40 · financial .25 · exposure .15 · age .10 · payment .10
BandsGREEN ≥75 · YELLOW 55–74.99 · RED ≤54.99
Auto vs manual (§3.11)exposure ≤500k · qty ≤10 · unit ≤100k → else manual review
The band is decided by this model — never copied from the bureau.
